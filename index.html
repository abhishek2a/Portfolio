About me

I studied commerce at the University of Kerala and chose accounting and finance as my direction. I'm now working through the ACCA qualification. I have completed 5 of the 13 papers and I'm studying Financial Reporting (FR), my sixth paper.

I'm happy to connect with recruiters, fellow ACCA students and anyone in accounting and finance.
